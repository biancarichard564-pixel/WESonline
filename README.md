Powering Purpose : Mapping Electricity Access and Community Opportunity in Imo State

Which Local Government Areas (LGAs) in Imo State are being left behind by limited electricity access? Have you ever wondered how these communities cope with reduced opportunities for education, healthcare, business growth, and digital inclusion?

Electricity is more than just power . It supports sustainable cities, strengthens critical infrastructure such as schools, health facilities and improves mobility by enhancing connectivity, safety, and economic activities across communities.

## Objective 
To map electricity access in relation to population, schools, roads, and critical infrastructure in Imo State, identifying communities where improved electricity access could enhance education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and overall community development.

## Problem
Reliable electricity remains a challenge for many LGA across Imo State, affecting communities, schools, and health facilities that depend on power for essential services.

## Method
All datasets were imported into QGIS and clipped to the Imo State boundary. The electricity raster was classified into Very Low, Low, Moderate, High and Very High electricity access categories. Settlement, school and health facility datasets were overlaid on the electricity layer and raster values were extracted to determine their level of electricity access. The resulting layers were classified and symbolised to identify communities and critical facilities located in underserved areas. Spatial patterns were then analysed to highlight locations where improved electricity access could enhance education, healthcare and community development opportunities.
